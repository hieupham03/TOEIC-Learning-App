## Restricted or unverified sources

- **ETS TOEIC Listening and Reading sample tests and official practice materials — `NOT_ALLOWED` for commercial offline bundling under the public terms.** ETS requires permission and an agreement to reproduce materials. The limited electronic grant described in its policy is for one year with territory and attribution requirements. The Listening and Reading licensing list names directions, sample questions, answer keys and the answer sheet. The separate Speaking and Writing entry names sound files. Obtain an exact audio, script and use grant before relying on any future license. Test-question and direction translations are excluded by the public policy. Trademark and logo permissions follow separate requirements. [ETS preparation listing](https://www.ets.org/toeic/test-takers/prepare.html), [ETS TOEIC licensing policy, effective June 2017](https://www.ets.org/legal/permissions/licensing.html), [ETS permission procedure](https://www.ets.org/legal/permissions/how-to-request.html)
- **Cambridge English B1 Preliminary Digital Listening Sample Test and Sample 1 tapescript — `NOT_ALLOWED` for this use on the public evidence.** Cambridge’s free preparation listing identifies the sample test and tapescript, and its copyright notice reserves rights in its examination, assessment and support material. The 2020 UCLES sample-test notice identifies copyright. No asset-specific permission to copy, adapt, bundle or commercially redistribute either component was established. A separately documented grant would need to cover credits, territories and expiry. [B1 Preliminary preparation](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/preliminary/preparation/index.aspx?level=independent&rows=24), [UCLES sample-test copyright notice, 2020](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/Images/577297-b1-preliminary-computer-based-sample-tests.pdf), [Cambridge copyright notice](https://www.cambridge.org/us/legal/copyright)
- **British Council LearnEnglish B1 “A phone call from a customer” — `NOT_ALLOWED` under the published terms.** Public listening and personal non-commercial copying with credit are permitted under the general terms. Adaptation, commercial use and incorporating the audio or transcript in a redistributable app require prior written approval. A future grant would need to identify the lesson version, components, credit, territory and term. [Lesson page](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/listening/b1/phone-call-customer), [British Council Terms of Use §10](https://www.britishcouncil.org/terms)
