# Stage 03 — B1 Source Rights Screening & Supplier Inquiry Brief V0.1

**Status:** request-for-information checklist only, NOT an outreach message sent or any binding procurement commitment. 

## 1. What qualifies as a supplier lead?
Supplier must show direct ownership/permission for the *specific* assets and the right to sublicense **two complete TOEIC-style (non-official) L&R practice test forms** for an Android educational app. Marketing claims of "official", “1000+ questions”, “API”, or “test preparation content” do not substitute for proof.

## 2. Requested factual fields in ONE batch
| Field | Required answer or artifact |
|---|---|
| Owner and provenance | Legal person owning/authoring questions, images, conversations, sounds, answer keys and explanations |
| Product inventory | Two unique 200-item L&R forms, blueprint by Part, separate practice-bank capacity, sample representative Part 1/3/7 (provided with permission) |
| Media | Audio files/scripts, picture licensing and graphic rights, transcript availability, formats; mapping documentation |
| Rights granted | Digital copy, edits/transformation, user-specific processing, hosting, caching, streaming, adaptation, private pilot 2–5, later public/commercial app (separate prices if necessary) |
| Restrictions | Territory (Vietnam/global), term, renewal/termination, attribution, brand/trademark usage, user caps, sub-processing, revocation/replacement rights |
| IP assurances | Ownership chain, third-party embedded rights, infringement claims and remedies |
| Quality | Reviewer credentials, key validation, listening alignment, correction process, replace-error SLA, approximate difficulty method |
| Commercial offer | Fixed license/setup fee, per learner/annual costs, 4-week pilot option, 12-month public option, support, quote validity and taxes |
| Delivery | Full assets + manifest for lawful ingestion, deadline, acceptance samples and right to reject failed QA |

## 3. Evidence statuses
- LEAD FOUND: public site mentions relevant services, **not a license**.
- CONTACTED: outgoing request logged, **no reply** (none contacted today).
- QUOTED: written complete scope received, **not agreement** (none today).
- RIGHTS VERIFIED: binding permission/contract plus chain for intended use (none today).
- QA PASSED: actual content checked independently (none today).

## 4. Possible supplier classes for research
1. Independent exam-content authors with clearly documented rights assignment (original, TOEIC-*style*), not necessarily a registered publisher.
2. English-test preparation publishers that own their questions and are willing to sign an app rehosting license (no named publisher currently verified to offer B1).
3. Bespoke partnership such as ETS Global describes, requiring direct written permission — no guarantee it can be replicated.
4. Licensed Creative Commons source creators for ancillary *generic* educational materials only; cannot assume full mock corpus exists.

## 5. Do not do without explicit owner authorisation
Do not scrape, upload/share owner PDFs, send external outreach as the Product Owner, purchase license, sign terms, or instruct a contractor to begin 400-item production. Developer account, trademarks and app store release also not approved.
