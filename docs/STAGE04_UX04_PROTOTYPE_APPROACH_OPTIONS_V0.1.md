# Stage 04 — UX-04 Prototype Approach Options V0.1

**State:** D04-IA-003 A approved **priority**, not approved UX-04 work. All options remain proposed.

## Common four-interaction baseline (non-negotiable)

| Focus | Primary candidate inherited from UX03 | Challenger | Controlled invariant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discoverability | **IA-A Task Hub** | IA-B Mode-centred | Practice and Full Mock equally findable; suggestions optional |
| Long Reading | **W-02A** named passage ↔ question with exact context return | W-02B accordion passage | Full figures, tables, references; no lost reading position |
| Exam state | **W-03A** compact contextual time/save/integrity | Expanded persistent status | No arbitrary pause/replay in comparable mock; no false save confirmation |
| Feedback | **W-04A** after practice or allowed mock completion | Batch feedback alternative | QA-vetted answers, no premature reveal, optional learner choice |

## Option A — Focused flows (analyst recommendation)

**Primary prototype:** a navigable candidate for IA-A with both entry actions, a Part7 excerpt/reference-return sequence, a mock entry and interruption scenario, and a post-attempt raw result/feedback screen-state sequence. 

**Limited challenger:** compare IA-B entry/discoverability and W-02B Reading accordion at two precise tasks; do not create and polish a complete second product UI.

**Decision evidence:** task-entry clarity, correct passage/reference return, understanding of 'Đang lưu' vs 'Đã lưu', and the user's interpretation of interrupted/noncomparable attempts.

**Trade-offs:** More efficient for directional research; may miss improvements available in full side-by-side comparison.

## Option B — Paired comparison

**Primary and challenger breadth equal** on entry, Reading and save/integrity. Keep mock behavior/policies identical across prototypes to avoid confounding. Alternate the order when conducting user walkthroughs.

**Evidence:** observed user preference plus task-completion observations, without claiming statistical generalizability (pilot 2–5).

**Trade-offs:** Higher design workload; risk of fatiguing learners and testing more than product scope warrants.

## Option C — Storyboard only

Document state transitions/copy and perform heuristics without an interactive prototype. Works to catch policy gaps but cannot substantiate actual reachability of navigation or passage-return interaction. Require later prototype approval separately.

## Recommendation and prohibited shortcuts

**Recommend Option A** because U1-A..U4-A are already approved as exploration direction and UX-03 selected A as the first comparison hypothesis, not final design. Preserve challenger screens/flows where they could reverse a decision.

None of the three variants authorizes a final navigation system, finished visual UI, software architecture, automated bulk content acquisition, code, paid tools or a Stage04 PASS. A prototype is a research artifact; it is not an executable Android application.
