## A. Content release/version evidence (B1 remains fixed)

| Model | Content correctness evidence | Benefits | Weaknesses and negative test | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C-A Version-frozen release sets** | A curated release of each form and dedicated Part bank is approved and identified by stable version; later amendments are separate | Easier to explain historical attempt-to-form relation; pre-exposure review clearer | Need correction/reissue process; wrong key found after release | QA-01..06 before content release |
| **C-B Controlled evolving catalog with traceable revisions** | Items/media/answer keys can receive traceable revisions; historical results remain bound to original version | More flexible correction process | Higher risk that a changed key changes historical score without notice; duplicate/leakage control harder | Complete provenance and correction audit |
| **C-C Reconciled release snapshots and amendment ledger** | Both release grouping and amendments are independently auditable | Balances stable tests and documented corrections | More administrative control and reconciliation; not proven | Later content governance and QA evidence |

**Common requirements:** 2×200 Full Mock B1 (PO-inventoried, not item-QA verified), form A/B independence, separate Part-practice bank, item/media/passage/audio/answer-map QA, no silently released missing media or key. Source-rights attested by PO; no re-inventory/proof request. No scraper/importer committed, no catalog structure chosen.

## B. Raw score and historical audit models

| Model | How to preserve meaning | Benefit | Risk and challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R-A Immutable-attempt interpretation** | Keep the attempt's item/key/form meaning attached to its original reported raw result | Strong historical readability | Correction presentation needs separate record |
| **R-B Recomputed but traceable interpretation** | Re-evaluate against corrected key while retaining original version/score and disclosure | Supports corrections | Risk of misleading “improved” score unless both histories and reasons are visible |

**Invariant:** Result displays correct/attempted per Part, 100 Listening, 100 Reading and total, status/date/form/version; no ETS-equivalent scaled score, no claim two A/B forms have equal difficulty. Duplicate submissions and unanswered items require tests at QA-07/08. Nothing here selects a scoring engine or database schema.
