## 3. Three alternative strategies, all **only proposals**

| UX-04 Option | Prototype breadth | Validation depth | Benefits | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Focused critical-path prototype (recommended)** | Explore IA-A / W-02A / W-03A / W-04A, compare IA-B + W-02B only at decision-critical moments | Desk heuristic + representative Android contexts + a small, targeted walkthrough only if a key ambiguity remains | Most efficient, protects B1 and avoids repetitive pilot tests | Less broad visual coverage; alternative challengers get smaller inspection |
| **B — Paired comparative prototypes** | Equivalent low-/mid-fidelity testable flows for IA-A vs IA-B and W-02A vs W-02B; other policies shared | Same scripted paired comparisons; balance order if testing people | Stronger comparative evidence | More prototype and review effort; potential fatigue in small pilot group |
| **C — Storyboard / desk validation first** | Screen-state storyboard and scenario walkthrough only (non-clickable), postpone interactive prototype until another decision | Requirement/state checklist and expert inspection only | Lowest effort for unresolved copy/state semantics | Cannot establish practical navigation or tap/reading usability; later interactive review still required |

No strategy is selected merely because it is recommended here. Authorizing A/B may allow producing **low- to mid-fidelity interactive prototypes** as design research after an explicit PO choice; it never authorizes app implementation, final UI baseline, architecture or code.

## 5. Prototype coverage boundaries

**In:** IA-A or IA-B as selected study approach; practice/one-item/Part choice; two mock A/B selection and pre-start rules; Listening/Reading phase signals; deliberate interruption examples; answer save pending/confirmed/error; Part7 multi-passage return; raw score and error review; family privacy/deletion; content unavailable. Use placeholders or licensed samples only as authorized.

**Not assumed built:** 120-minute working timer, synchronized audio backend, persistent accounts, official score conversion, real save/recovery engine, 400-item content importer, automated testing pipeline, server/backend. Prototype may simulate state to assess copy and comprehension; clearly label simulations.

## 6. Decision and evidence principles

- Validate **comprehension and discoverability**, not claimed statistical success or TOEIC score gain.
- Predefine observer prompts, tasks and what counts as a confusing state; do not prescribe final usability success thresholds silently.
- No invented count of participants, trial duration, device models or positive feedback. All quantities are plans, not measured results.
- Critical error = risk of losing acknowledged answer, misleadingly showing mock as comparable, exposing protected answer mid-mock, exposing other learner's record, hiding a missing passage, or falsely claiming official TOEIC score. Proposed action: no design sign-off until handled or consciously risk-accepted by PO at the appropriate Gate.
- Accessible wording and non-color-only saving/error distinctions; long Reading retains complete tables/figures and the last useful passage context. Any numeric accessibility criteria are proposals for review, not baseline amendments.
