# Stage 03 — B1 Two-Full-Mock Content Workplan & Quality Gates V0.1

**Status:** proposed process and acceptance standards only; no questions/assets authored, imported or audited. Two forms mandatory for B1; not equivalent to official ETS score certification.

## 1. Form manifest blueprint
| TOEIC L&R Part | Form A | Form B | Total Q | Required grouped media |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| P1 — Photographs | 6 | 6 | 12 | 12 photos + appropriate audio prompts and distractors |
| P2 — Question–Response | 25 | 25 | 50 | 50 listening prompts with spoken answer choices |
| P3 — Conversations | 39 | 39 | 78 | 13 conversations per form, 3 questions per conversation; required graphic(s) as relevant |
| P4 — Talks | 30 | 30 | 60 | 10 talks per form, 3 questions per talk; graphic(s) as relevant |
| P5 — Incomplete Sentences | 30 | 30 | 60 | 60 standalone question and key sets |
| P6 — Text Completion | 16 | 16 | 32 | 4 texts per form with 4 questions each |
| P7 — Reading Comprehension | 54 | 54 | 108 | Reading passage sets, single/multiple documents, question–passage traceability |
| **TOTAL** | **200** | **200** | **400** | 200 Listening + 200 Reading items |

Use official ETS format as structure, NOT ETS creative content. 2 forms = distinct item IDs, questions, source passages, audio and answer keys. Original content generation must avoid reproducing or paraphrasing protected published questions too closely.

## 2. Multi-gate workflow, not a claim of completion
| Gate | What to check | Required evidence | Current |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q0 Rights | Claim of original authorship / license and use scope for **each** item and media asset | Creator/contract, permission IDs, provenance and expiry | MISSING |
| Q1 Manifest | Exactly 200 items/form, Part distribution, unique IDs, asset links | Validated A/B manifest exports, count report | MISSING |
| Q2 Content validity | Correct keys, plausible distractors, no ambiguous wording, correct scripts | Reviewer rubric + correction tickets | MISSING |
| Q3 Media | Audio transcript consistency, correct group/order, understandable speech, visual rights | Full asset playback/mapping check results | MISSING |
| Q4 Overlap | No A/B duplicate or leaked practice-bank items; prevent re-exposure before final mock | Similarity checks + reviewer sign-off + content separation policy | MISSING |
| Q5 Difficulty/score | Balance Part/topic/difficulty; compare forms only cautiously | Competent examiner review, score-report caveat | MISSING |
| Q6 Pilot release | No open known critical rights/item/audio defects; rollback/retraction process | QA approval sign-off and release register | MISSING |

Proposed quality bar: **100%** of the intended 400 items and associated assets receive rights/source and key/media validation, and **zero open known critical faults** before content distribution. 100% means coverage of review, NOT scientifically provable zero error. Propose two passes: author self-review plus a second qualified reviewer; with one person only, risk remains escalated to PO rather than misreported as independently reviewed.

## 3. Score validity distinction
- Display `correct/attempted`, percentage correct and Part breakdown as **practice statistics** if PO approves.
- Do **not** label as ETS official scaled scores, actual improvement of 50–100 TOEIC points, or precise equivalent of certified examination scores; different uncalibrated forms may vary in difficulty.
- Even a positive raw difference between Mock A/B for 2–5 users is **descriptive**, not causal evidence that the app raised proficiency.
- Need PO to approve the precise wording of four-week pilot KPI before requirements baseline. B1's two mocks remain mandatory, no automatic KPI downgrading.

## 4. Pilot content separation
`Form A pre` / `Form B post` / `Practice Part bank` are distinct sets (unless PO explicitly accepts a different evidence model and its limitations). 400 form items alone do **not** cover all practice-bank needs. Each item's ownership, license, status, version and release scope are recorded.
