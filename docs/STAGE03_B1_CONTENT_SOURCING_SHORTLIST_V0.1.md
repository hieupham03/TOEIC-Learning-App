# Stage 03 — B1 Content Sourcing Shortlist V0.1

**Updated:** 2026-11-21. **State:** desk research completed; no provider contract, no rights transfer, no purchase. **Decision:** D03-LOCK-001=B1 approved, MVP LOCK=HOLD. CP-01–CP-05 active.

## 1. Main finding
No independently confirmed, turnkey catalogue currently found that supplies **two legally rehostable, nonoverlapping TOEIC-style full forms (400 items) with matching audio, pictures, texts, keys** for this 2–5 learner Android pilot. This is a statement about *research evidence*, not proof that such products do not exist. Do not copy a public practice site simply because its content is viewable.

## 3. Distinguish supplier types
- An app/site with its own questions is **a competitor/reference**, not automatically a supplier.
- A company offering B2B assessment software or imports does **not** necessarily license TOEIC-style items or allow our rehosting. Confirm a SKU or contract.
- A publisher selling student books normally sells a **personal copy**, not app distribution rights.
- ETS's publicly posted sample-question licensing fees are NOT a quote for 400 items or two full test forms. ETS lists entire disclosed forms and individual test questions as unavailable under its published ordinary licensing programme.
- The ETS+digiSchool+Hachette arrangement evidences a specific partnership. It does not imply third parties qualify or that the same data can be sublicensed to us.

## 4. Recommendation for PO (not scope lock)
**P1 + P2 in parallel**: work out an original production route and a supplier outreach/rights negotiation route; proceed only with rights-cleared original or specially licensed material. Do not pay or begin production without PO authorisation. Keep user-supplied books as 'candidate—not cleared'.
