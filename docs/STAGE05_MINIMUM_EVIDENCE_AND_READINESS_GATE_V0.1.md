## C. Levels of possible future outcomes

- **NOT RUN** without actual authorized test; **BLOCKED** when preconditions/approval missing.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** after an authorized observation with insufficient/correlated/conflicting proof.
- **FAIL** only after a credible falsifier under signed scope; **PASS (bounded)** only after actual QA-signed evidence meets applicable approved criteria in the specified context.
- **NOT APPLICABLE (reasoned)** requires explicit PO/QA rationale and does not remove B1, AT or CP requirements by silence.
